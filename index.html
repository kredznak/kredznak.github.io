What I make

One tool, one job. I would rather build something that does a single thing well than something that does six things adequately. Where the work can happen on your own machine, it does — and none of my add-ons ask you to make an account.

Smart Brand Stylist

Turns a logo or a website address into a usable brand kit — colors and font pairings — then keeps the rest of your design in line with it. It will also suggest copy in your brand voice, and find and fix off-brand colors and fonts already on the page. Logo colors are read in the panel, on your device; the image is never uploaded.

How I handle your data

As little of it as possible. My add-ons have no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing you put into them is stored on my side. Where a feature genuinely needs a server — reading a public web page, or asking a language model for copy — the request is forwarded and the reply returned, and that is the end of it. Each add-on links its own full privacy policy above.
